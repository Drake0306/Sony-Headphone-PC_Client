Hairline, with the Panel controls

Every screen of the app in the merged design. The base is Hairline: off-white paper, Forest Gray, dot-matrix numerals, one-pixel rules. From Panel it takes the boxed switches, the Link list and the fader equalizer. Orange appears in exactly two places, the active ULT level and "playing", so it means "live".

NC / AMB / OFF switchPanel's boxed buttons replace Hairline's thin segment, in the popover and in the window.
ULT switchPanel's boxed OFF / 1 / 2 with the orange active box, in both places.
Ambient levelStays Hairline's tick bar, not the knob. Shown larger in the Noise screen.
BatteryStays Hairline's dot-matrix number.
Link listPanel's two-column list with "playing" is now on the Overview and in the popover footer.
EqualizerPanel's faders replace the bar chart: mini on the Overview, full size with dB marks on the Sound screen.
CodecGets its own boxed row: codec, sample rate, bitrate, DSEE, and the connection-quality switch the device supports.
Everything elseHairline as drawn before.

Control states

Every control shows what the headphones have actually confirmed. No existing client does this, and it's the main reason their controls "snap back".

Confirmed
OFF12

Solid fill. The headphones replied with this value.

Pending
OFF12

Striped fill for up to a second while the command is in flight.

Refused
OFF12

Orange outline and a one-line reason: "ULT is off during calls."
