Flair themes

Six louder appearances for SHDC, Saturday 8 November 2026. Unlike the ten palette themes, these change more than colours: a wash over the paper, a colour per equalizer band, a second and third accent, and a glow on live controls. The type, the rules and the controls stay Hairline underneath, so they still read as the same app.

What these need beyond a palette

The ten themes shipped in 0.4.0 are eight tokens each. A flair theme adds up to eleven more and a few component rules, all optional, so every existing theme keeps working unchanged:

TokenWhat it doesUsed by
--washA gradient or texture laid over the paper, behind every surface. Text never sits on it directly: rows, the popover and the screens keep a flat --box behind them.Synthwave, Riso, Aurora, Sunset
--accent-2, --accent-3Second and third live colours: the battery numeral, the ambient ticks, the second-state box.all six
--band-1 … --band-6One colour per equalizer band (knob, fill, value). Defaults to the accent.all six
--glowA soft shadow in the accent colour behind live controls and numerals. Off (none) in every palette theme.Synthwave, Aurora, Acid
  • The Advanced equalizer's ten bands take their colours from the six band tokens, interpolated, so a rainbow runs across all ten.
  • Pending stripes and refused outlines keep their meaning: stripes stay ink and stripe, refused stays the first accent. Nothing live loses its signal to decoration.
  • Riso's grain and Synthwave's grid are CSS gradients, no images, so the bundle does not grow.
  • Contrast: Acid and Sunset were tuned so ink on paper stays above 7:1 and muted text above 4.5:1; the wash never sits behind text.
  • Cost: one day of work including tests; the ten palette themes needed none of these rules, so this is a separate step after you pick.

Built

All six shipped in SHDC 0.5.0 on 8 November 2026, with the token table above as implemented (the fill of each fader follows its band colour). The three second accents were darkened for contrast before building: Riso orange to #d64400, Sunset amber to #8e4b00, Spectrum green to #00805a.
