Theme options

Ten appearances for SHDC beyond Paper and Ink, Saturday 8 November 2026. Each card paints the real popover and a slice of the Sound screen with that theme's six tokens. Nothing else changes: the type, the one-pixel rules, the boxed switches and the faders stay Hairline.

Switch to compare. The app marks "live" with orange today; a theme can keep that or bring its signature colour.

How I chose

Five light, five dark, so System mode has a matching pair whichever way you go. The seven named palettes are the ones that turn up most in 2026 editor and terminal theme lists: Catppuccin is the most installed Neovim scheme and the one most often recommended as a default, Tokyo Night and Nord have the longest install histories on the VS Code marketplace, and Gruvbox, Dracula, Solarized and Rosé Pine ship bundled in most multi-theme tools. The three others answer requests that come up in app settings everywhere: a true-black OLED theme (black pixels are off, so it also saves power on OLED screens), a sepia reading theme, and a warm brown paper that follows this app's own metaphor.

What I did not include: a high-contrast accessibility theme (worth doing, but as a separate setting that also thickens rules and enlarges type rather than as a colour swap), and more than one variant of the same family (Catppuccin has four flavours, Rosé Pine three), to keep the picker readable.

What adding them means

  • Each theme is one block of six values in tokens.css and one entry in the Appearance picker. No component changes.
  • The Appearance row has three boxes today (Paper, Ink, System). Twelve do not fit on one row, so the picker becomes a two-row grid or a small list with a swatch per theme. I will show the control before building it.
  • System keeps mapping to Paper and Ink. A later option could let you pick which light and which dark theme System switches between.
  • The QR code stays dark on light in every theme, as it does in Ink today, so phones can scan it.
  • On the accent: themes marked "own accent" look like their namesakes with it. If you prefer one orange everywhere, say so and every theme keeps ULT orange.
