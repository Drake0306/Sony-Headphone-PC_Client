How Scenes work

A scene is a name, a trigger, and a set of actions. Triggers: by hand, headphones connect, a time range (wraps midnight), an app in front, or the microphone in use. Every two seconds the first enabled scene in list order that matches becomes active, and its actions are sent once. Each action is one of your existing controls: noise mode, ambient level and voice focus, ULT, an EQ preset, volume, DSEE. "Keep" leaves a setting alone. Nothing new goes to the headphones, so the deny-list and the confirmation flow are untouched.

Four scenes ship switched off so the screen is not empty: At the desk On a call Late Walking. Enable the ones you want and change them freely. Your headphones were not touched overnight; the device was disconnected the whole time and no setting was written.
